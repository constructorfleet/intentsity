                                                <h2>Content fundamentals — voice &amp; copy</h2>
                                                <ul data-tight="true">
                                                    <li>
                                                        <p>
                                                            <strong>Tone:</strong>
                                                             engineering-direct. "127 clips queued." "Precision rose 1.4%." Never marketing-inflected, never celebratory.
                                                        </p>
                                                    </li>
                                                    <li>
                                                        <p>
                                                            <strong>Person:</strong>
                                                             third-person or imperative. Not "you", not "we". "Save labels", not "Save your labels".
                                                        </p>
                                                    </li>
                                                    <li>
                                                        <p>
                                                            <strong>Casing:</strong>
                                                            <strong>Sentence case for buttons and labels</strong>
                                                             ("Save labels", "Queue fine-tune"). Title Case reserved for section headers on marketing surfaces (none in the current product). UPPERCASE for eyebrow labels only (
                                                            <code>SESSION</code>
                                                            , 
                                                            <code>ARGUMENTS</code>
                                                            ) with 
                                                            <code>--tracking-caps</code>
                                                            .
                                                        </p>
                                                    </li>
                                                    <li>
                                                        <p>
                                                            <strong>Emoji:</strong>
                                                             never in product UI. Not in toasts, not in empty states, not in comments. Tools like this are used all day; emoji become noise.
                                                        </p>

                                                <h2>Visual foundations</h2>
                                                <p>
                                                    <strong>Colors.</strong>
                                                     Cool graphite neutrals with a 
                                                    <strong>signal-cyan</strong>
                                                     brand (
                                                    <code>--brand-500 #12a394</code>
                                                    ) and a 
                                                    <strong>five-color annotation palette</strong>
                                                     that IS the semantic palette — TP emerald, TN blue, FP red, FN amber, BG violet. Same colors drive Toast tones and status dots. Full palette in 
                                                    <code>tokens/colors.css</code>
                                                    .
                                                </p>
                                                <p>
                                                    <strong>Typography.</strong>
                                                    <code>IBM Plex Sans</code>
                                                     for UI, 
                                                    <code>IBM Plex Mono</code>
                                                     for data, IDs, JSON, and keyboard hints. Mono is used 
                                                    <em>heavily</em>
                                                     — it's how you tell at a glance that a value is machine-precise. 
                                                    <strong>⚠︎ Substitution flag:</strong>
                                                     no source assets were provided; Plex was chosen for its engineering/tooling associations. If the real product uses another face (Söhne, Inter, SF Pro, GT America…) please send the font files and this pair will be swapped.
                                                </p>
                                                <p>
                                                    <strong>Spacing.</strong>
                                                     14-step ramp on a 2px baseline — 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 80, 96.
                                                </p>
                                                <p>
                                                    <strong>Radii.</strong>
                                                     Small and functional: 
                                                    <code>sm 3</code>
                                                    , 
                                                    <code>md 5</code>
                                                    , 
                                                    <code>lg 8</code>
                                                    , 
                                                    <code>xl 12</code>
                                                    , 
                                                    <code>2xl 16</code>
                                                    , 
                                                    <code>pill</code>
                                                    . 
                                                    <strong>No large radii on cards.</strong>
                                                     Panels stay square-ish; only avatars and badges get 
                                                    <code>--r-pill</code>
                                                    .
                                                </p>
                                                <p>
                                                    <strong>Elevation.</strong>
                                                     Mostly flat. Panels are separated by 1px borders (
                                                    <code>--border-subtle</code>
                                                    ), not shadows. Shadows appear only on true overlays: dropdowns, tooltips, dialogs, toasts. No inner glows. No colored shadows.
                                                </p>
                                                <p>
                                                    <strong>Borders.</strong>
                                                    <code>--border-subtle</code>
                                                     for panel/row separation, 
                                                    <code>--border-default</code>
                                                     for inputs, 
                                                    <code>--border-strong</code>
                                                     for check/radio idle. Focus rings are a 3px 
                                                    <code>--brand-500</code>
                                                    -at-28% halo, never a solid outline. No colored left-border-only cards.
                                                </p>
                                                <p>
                                                    <strong>Backgrounds.</strong>
                                                     No gradients. No photo backdrops. No hand-drawn illustrations. No repeating patterns. The one gradient this system uses is the 
                                                    <strong>transparent overlay for dialogs</strong>
                                                     (
                                                    <code>--surface-overlay</code>
                                                     at 55% + 4px backdrop blur). Full-bleed color appears only in 
                                                    <code>thumbnail.html</code>
                                                    .
                                                </p>
                                                <p>
                                                    <strong>Animation.</strong>
                                                     Fast and unfussy. 
                                                    <code>--dur-fast 140ms</code>
                                                     for hovers, 
                                                    <code>--dur-med 220ms</code>
                                                     for panel/state changes. Easing is 
                                                    <code>cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)</code>
                                                    . 
                                                    <strong>No bounces</strong>
                                                    , no springs (except a rare micro-detail via 
                                                    <code>--ease-spring</code>
                                                     — never on layout). Buttons scale to 
                                                    <code>.98</code>
                                                     on press. Intentsity playhead is a plain interval, not a tween.
                                                </p>
                                                <p>
                                                    <strong>Hover / press.</strong>
                                                     Hover = one step warmer surface (
                                                    <code>--surface-hover</code>
                                                    ), no color shift. Press = next step (
                                                    <code>--surface-active</code>
                                                    ) 
                                                    <em>plus</em>
                                                    <code>scale(.98)</code>
                                                     on buttons. Ghost buttons rely entirely on background change; they never gain a border on hover.
                                                </p>
                                                <p>
                                                    <strong>Transparency &amp; blur.</strong>
                                                     Only for dialog overlays. Sidebars, popovers, tooltips are 
                                                    <strong>fully opaque</strong>
                                                     — this is professional software; users need reliable contrast against arbitrary content.
                                                </p>
                                                <p>
                                                    <strong>Iconography.</strong>
                                                     Currently uses 
                                                    <strong>inline stroke SVG</strong>
                                                     at 1.5px, 14–16px viewbox, drawn in place — see the 
                                                    <code>Ico</code>
                                                     helpers in the UI kit JSX. 
                                                    <strong>No icon font is bundled yet.</strong>
                                                     The nearest CDN match if you need more coverage is 
                                                    <strong>Lucide</strong>
                                                     (
                                                    <code>https://unpkg.com/lucide-static</code>
                                                    ), same 1.5px stroke weight. Substitution flag — please supply the real icon set if one exists.
                                                </p>
                                                <p>
                                                    <strong>Imagery.</strong>
                                                     None. This is a data tool. If a marketing surface is ever needed, imagery should be 
                                                    <strong>cool-toned, high-contrast, subtly grainy</strong>
                                                     — waveform close-ups, oscilloscope shots, studio-monitor macros — not people or lifestyle photography.
                                                </p>
                                                <p>
                                                    <strong>Cards.</strong>
                                                     1px 
                                                    <code>--border-subtle</code>
                                                    , 
                                                    <code>--r-lg (8px)</code>
                                                    , 
                                                    <code>--surface-panel</code>
                                                     fill, no shadow by default. Header strip has its own 1px bottom border; footer strip is 
                                                    <code>--surface-sunken</code>
                                                    .
                                                </p>
                                                <p>
                                                    <strong>Layout rules.</strong>
                                                     Fixed left sidebar (232px), fixed top toolbar (48px), everything else flexes. Content zones separated by 1px borders — never gaps. Right inspector panels are 280px. Content max-widths only exist inside long-form docs (none in the current product).
                                                </p>
